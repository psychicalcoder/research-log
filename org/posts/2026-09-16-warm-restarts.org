#+TITLE: Warm restarts do not help at this scale
#+DATE: <2026-09-16 Wed>
#+DESCRIPTION: Cosine schedules with restarts versus a single decay, on the 40M-parameter run.

Last week's schedule sweep left one open question: whether the small but consistent gap between the cosine-with-restarts runs and the single-cycle runs was real or noise. I re-ran both with five seeds each. It is real, and it goes the wrong way.

* Setup

The model and data are unchanged from the [[file:2026-09-02-baseline.org][baseline]]. The only variable is the learning-rate schedule. Both schedules share the peak rate $\eta_{\max} = 6 \times 10^{-4}$ and a linear warmup of 2,000 steps. After warmup, the single-cycle schedule follows

\begin{equation}
\eta_t = \eta_{\min} + \tfrac{1}{2}(\eta_{\max} - \eta_{\min})\left(1 + \cos\frac{\pi t}{T}\right),
\end{equation}

while the restart schedule resets $t$ to zero every $T_i$ steps, with $T_{i+1} = 2 T_i$ and $T_0 = 10{,}000$. Everything else, including the data order per seed, is held fixed[fn:1].

* Results

The restart runs recover quickly after each reset, but never quite catch up. At 100k steps the gap is 0.018 nats, roughly three standard deviations.

#+CAPTION: Final validation loss at 100k steps.
| Schedule            | Seeds | Val. loss | Std.  |
|---------------------+-------+-----------+-------|
| Single cosine       |     5 |     2.611 | 0.006 |
| Cosine w/ restarts  |     5 |     2.629 | 0.007 |
| Constant (control)  |     3 |     2.688 | 0.011 |

I had expected the opposite. The usual argument is that restarts escape sharp minima; the counter-argument, which I now find more convincing, is that at this batch size we are nowhere near a minimum for most of training, and every reset simply throws away progress along the low-curvature directions.

#+BEGIN_QUOTE
If the loss landscape is a long, gently sloping valley, kicking the optimiser back up the wall is not exploration. It is a detour.
#+END_QUOTE

* Reproduction

The sweep is one call; the seeds are baked into the config.

#+BEGIN_SRC python
from lab import run, Schedule

for seed in range(5):
    run(config="cfg/40m.yaml",
        schedule=Schedule.cosine(peak=6e-4, warmup=2_000, total=100_000),
        seed=seed, tag="single")
    run(config="cfg/40m.yaml",
        schedule=Schedule.restarts(peak=6e-4, warmup=2_000, t0=10_000, mult=2),
        seed=seed, tag="restart")
#+END_SRC
